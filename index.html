About Us

About Valmo Delivery Franchise

A trusted logistics partnership built on Meesho's nationwide order network.

Valmo Delivery Franchise is a delivery-hub partnership programme created for entrepreneurs who want to build a real logistics business on top of a proven, high-volume e-commerce network. As a partner, you operate a local delivery hub that receives, sorts, dispatches and delivers Meesho orders within your pincode cluster — while Valmo and Meesho take care of the technology, the order flow and the brand trust.

Our mission is simple: make first-generation entrepreneurs franchise-ready. From site selection and hub setup to staff training, standard operating procedures and day-to-day operations support, we stand with you at every step so your hub goes live quickly and runs profitably from the very first month.

Today our partners serve 17,000+ pincodes across India and process millions of shipments every month. Whether you are an existing courier operator, a small business owner or a first-time investor, Valmo gives you a low-investment, high-return entry point into India's fast-growing e-commerce logistics market.

  • End-to-end setup support — location survey, hub design and launch planning
  • Complete training for you and your delivery staff before go-live
  • Transparent, volume-based payouts with real-time performance dashboards
  • Dedicated relationship manager and 24/7 operational helpdesk
